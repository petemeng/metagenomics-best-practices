Article 15 data and database notice
===================================

Biological input
----------------

ERR9765746 is the real paired Illumina run for the 71-strain MOCK1 DNA
material described by Meslier et al. (2022,
doi:10.1038/s41597-022-01762-z), project PRJEB52977 and sample
SAMEA14435832. Article 15 starts from all 99,991 synchronized read pairs
retained by the fixed Article 13 fastp branch from the first 100,000 complete
pairs in the checksum-identified ENA files. This is a laboratory community,
not a natural microbiome and not a cohort from which prevalence, association,
causality, or ecological generalization can be inferred.

The clean input contains 199,982 mate records. Twenty-two R1 records and 31 R2
records are 50--69 bp long; the fixed MetaPhlAn --read_min_len 70 rule therefore
passes 199,929 reads into mapping and abundance estimation. Input reads and
profiling reads are reported as separate denominators.

No raw or cleaned FASTQ is committed. The ignored files are tied to the
Article 13 evidence by compressed and uncompressed SHA-256, record counts,
base counts, and a normalized paired-read-ID hash. Routine QA reads only the
small frozen profile and aggregate evidence.

marker support, normalized logs, resource measurements, and checksums.

The release-specific PKL contains 72,000 SGB taxonomy entries: 27,891 are
known SGBs and 44,109 are unknown SGBs under the database species-label rule.
It contains 13,907,686 marker records. These catalog counts belong to
mpa_vJan26_CHOCOPhlAnSGB_202605 and must not be replaced with the smaller
catalog counts reported for the database generation used in the 2023 method
paper.

Interpretation boundary
-----------------------

MetaPhlAn estimates community composition from clade-specific marker support.
Its relative abundance is not an absolute cell count, biomass measurement,
viability assay, transcriptional activity measurement, or strain-transmission
result. MetaPhlAn 4.2 estimates an UNCLASSIFIED fraction by default; a profile
generated with --skip_unclassified_estimation instead renormalizes reported
clades internally. Those denominators are kept separate.

An SGB whose species label contains _SGB is retained as an unknown SGB rather
than assigned a fabricated species name. Marker breadth, coverage, estimated
read support, sequencing depth, controls, and post-profile threshold
sensitivity are reported alongside relative abundance. The --perc_nonzero
default of 0.33 belongs to quasi-marker disambiguation and is not presented as
a universal taxon-detection limit.
