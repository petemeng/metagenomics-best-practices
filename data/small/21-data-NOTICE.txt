Article 21 data and software notice
===================================

Taxonomic and functional tables
--------------------------------
Article 21 reuses checksum-locked, publishable small outputs from Articles 15,
16, 19, and 20. Their source accessions, software/database versions, licenses,
and exclusions remain governed by the corresponding data-NOTICE files and
checksum manifests. No FASTQ, database index, per-read classification, or
sample-specific search index is duplicated here.

Genome-equivalent calibration
-----------------------------
The one-time calibration uses the same ERR9765746 MOCK1 clean sequence universe
as Articles 15 and 19: 99,991 paired fragments / 199,982 reads after the Article
13 fastp run. Raw and clean FASTQ files remain excluded from Git.

MicrobeCensus is obtained from the official GPL-3.0 repository at Git tag
v1.1.1, commit dfc42d356bfd7943633cde6c0fbfc0b116f29ae2:
https://github.com/snayfach/MicrobeCensus/tree/v1.1.1

The tagged source still reports internal version 1.1.0. The Article 21 wrapper
does not change the marker database, alignment command, thresholds, weights, or
average-genome-size algorithm. It only replaces a Python 3-incompatible bytes
comparison in the RAPsearch2 v2.15 version preflight with text decoding.

MicrobeCensus citation:
Nayfach S, Pollard KS. Average genome size estimation improves comparative
metagenomics and sheds light on the functional ecology of the human microbiome.
Genome Biology. 2015;16:51. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-015-0611-7

Interpretation boundary
-----------------------
Genome equivalents equal total sequenced base pairs divided by estimated
average genome size. They represent aggregate microbial genome coverage in this
sequencing library. They are not cell counts, organism counts, biomass, copies
per gram, or an absolute concentration. RPKG is used only when gene RPK and the
genome-equivalent estimate come from the same filtered sequence universe.

The current deterministic MOCK1 prefix contains fewer than the two million
reads recommended by the MicrobeCensus documentation for generally accurate
AGS estimation. The 150-bp result is therefore a method calibration, accompanied
by a 100-bp sensitivity branch, and is not presented as a high-precision
ecological estimate.
