Interpretation boundary
-----------------------

The assay is MetaPhlAn-derived relative abundance expressed on an
approximately 0-100 percentage scale. Its sample sums range from 94.65166 to
100.00003 because taxa without a matching rowTree tip are removed by the
package constructor. These values are not raw read counts. The number_reads
field in colData is sequencing-depth metadata and must not be substituted for
feature-level counts without an explicitly justified reconstruction.
