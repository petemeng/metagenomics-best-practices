Article 11 environment and database evidence
============================================

Validation date: 2026-07-19

This article separates three evidence classes:

1. LIVE_ENVIRONMENT
   The two conda environments were solved and installed on native Linux.
   Direct package versions, executable entry points, explicit Linux-64 package
   locks, and built-in installation tests are captured once and then frozen.

2. MANIFEST_ONLY
   Release-specific database identities and upstream checksums are recorded,
   but the multi-gigabyte archives are not committed to Git and are not
   downloaded by routine QA.

3. BLOCKED_NO_UPSTREAM_CHECKSUM
   HUMAnN's official full-database endpoints are mutable and do not publish a
   release checksum in the user manual. They remain fail-closed until an
   administrator stages the archive, records its retrieval date and response
   metadata, computes a local SHA-256, and stores it in an immutable
   institution-controlled location.

The MEGAHIT and SPAdes built-in tests are software installation checks, not
biological validation and not performance benchmarks. Database-dependent
MetaPhlAn, HUMAnN, CheckM2, and GTDB-Tk analyses remain NOT_DOWNLOADED or
NOT_RUN until their exact database releases pass their own validation query.
