Article 14 data, reference, and privacy notice
================================================

Human positive control
----------------------

ERR194147 is the real paired Illumina WGS accession used as the NA12878
human-read benchmark in the Hostile paper (Constantinides et al., 2023,
doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/btad728). ENA identifies the run as PRJEB3381,
SAMEA1573618, paired WGS on Illumina HiSeq 2000. Article 14 streams only the
first 20,000 complete synchronized records from each checksum-identified mate
file into Git-ignored, access-controlled scratch.

Public availability is not treated as anonymity. No ERR194147 sequence, read
header, genotype, phenotype, or individual-level inference is committed or
published by this repository. Only aggregate method-control counts, normalized
logs, resource measurements, and cryptographic fingerprints are frozen.
Clinical, cohort, and otherwise controlled human FASTQ must follow the consent,
ethics, institutional, jurisdictional, and repository-access conditions that
apply to the source project; this public benchmark is not permission to release
other human sequence data.

Microbial retention control
----------------------------

ERR9765746 is the real paired Illumina run for the 71-strain MOCK1 DNA material
described by Meslier et al. (2022, doi:10.1038/s41597-022-01762-z), project
PRJEB52977 and sample SAMEA14435832. Article 14 independently streams the first
20,000 complete synchronized records per mate. It is used only to measure
microbial retention and preprocessing sensitivity, not to infer a natural
community.

Reference
---------

The Hostile 2.0.2 default Bowtie2 index human-t2t-hla combines
T2T-CHM13v2.0 with IPD-IMGT/HLA v3.51 and is labeled 2023-07 by the official
release. The official tar SHA-256 is:

5b584f5c28abeec5dba78bd37b53fa476dd42af57051d2fb7d2f2098e3a2df13

The multi-gigabyte index and all FASTQ files remain outside Git. The one-time
run records the archive size, verifies the official checksum, and writes a
separate SHA-256 manifest for every extracted Bowtie2 index file. Routine QA
uses only the frozen aggregate evidence and never accesses ENA, object storage,
or FASTQ.

Interpretation boundary
-----------------------

Hostile removes a complete pair when either mate maps to the human index and
retains a pair only when both mates are unmapped. Low-complexity filtering and
sequence-exact deduplication are separate sensitivity branches. Sequence
identity alone does not prove PCR duplication, optical duplication, or a UMI
family, and the primary Article 14 output applies neither low-complexity
filtering nor deduplication.
