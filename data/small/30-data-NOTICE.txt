Article 30 data notice
======================

Study and biological material
-----------------------------
Meslier V, Quinquis B, Da Silva K, et al. Benchmarking second and
third-generation sequencing platforms for microbial metagenomics.
Scientific Data. 2022;9:694. DOI: 10.1038/s41597-022-01762-z.

The study constructed uneven synthetic microbial DNA communities. Article 30
uses the official ENA Illumina HiSeq 3000 paired runs for MOCK1 (71 strains;
SAMEA14435832; ERR9765746) and MOCK2 (87 strains; SAMEA14435833;
ERR9765747) from project PRJEB52977. These are sequenced biological mock
materials, not simulated reads. They are controlled benchmarks and do not
represent the strain complexity, host DNA, mobile elements, or unknown
sequence space of a natural metagenome.

Accession reconciliation
------------------------
The publication's Table 4 prints ERR9765446 and ERR9765447 for MOCK1 and
MOCK2. The official ENA project metadata checked on 2026-07-25 identify the
Illumina runs as ERR9765746 and ERR9765747. Article 30 uses the official ENA
identifiers, URLs, byte counts, MD5 values, read counts, and base counts and
retains the publication-versus-archive discrepancy in the audit trail.

Storage and deterministic selection
-----------------------------------
The four full FASTQ archives are downloaded into data/raw/article30/full/ and
are excluded from Git. Each archive must match the ENA byte count and MD5 in
30-source-manifest.tsv. Exactly 2,000,000 synchronized read pairs per mock are
then selected without replacement while streaming the complete archives. The
sequential sampling algorithm is seeded with 20260730 and namespaced by mock;
selected records remain in original run order. Selected and fastp-cleaned
FASTQ files also remain outside Git.

Assembly scope and reuse limits
-------------------------------
The frozen comparison is a bounded workflow demonstration: MEGAHIT 1.2.9 and
metaSPAdes/SPAdes 4.3.0 are run on each two-million-pair subset separately and
on the two subsets together. Co-assembly therefore combines two deliberately
different mock compositions and twice the input read budget. It is not an
algorithm-only benchmark and cannot establish a universal winner. Contiguity,
read recruitment, runtime, and memory are reported together; N50 alone is not
treated as correctness. Reference-aware assembly accuracy is reserved for the
assembly-QC chapter.

Raw-read terms remain those of the European Nucleotide Archive and the source
study. Repository text and original redraws are released under this project's
licenses; third-party reads are not relicensed or redistributed through Git.
