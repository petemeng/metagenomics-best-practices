MUClite is a real 16S rRNA V4 ASV object distributed with decontam. Article
07 uses it only to execute and inspect the statistical classifier. It is not
presented as a shotgun abundance table. decontam itself accepts marker-gene
and metagenomic feature tables, including taxonomic groups and MAGs, provided
that matching DNA-quantitation data or sequenced negative controls exist.

The executable environment is R 4.4.1, Bioconductor 3.19 and decontam 1.24.0.
The current Bioconductor release checked on 2026-07-19 was 3.23 with decontam
1.32.0 and R 4.6. The older package is retained to reproduce the exact
MUClite object and validated results; the difference is disclosed rather than
silently described as the current release.

2. Salter shotgun dilution evidence
-----------------------------------

File: 07-salter-shotgun-evidence.tsv

Source: Salter SJ et al. Reagent and laboratory contamination can critically
impact sequence-based microbiome analyses. BMC Biology. 2014;12:87.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1186/s12915-014-0087-z
PMCID: PMC4228153
License: CC BY 4.0.

The four rows transcribe the extraction-kit names and qualitative Results /
Figure 3 statements. The paper reports that contamination predominated by the
fourth ten-fold dilution (approximately 10^4 Salmonella cells) for every kit
and that non-Salmonella family profiles differed by kit. No pixel-derived
abundance or unreported error statistic is introduced. The 10^4-cell result
is an experiment-specific anchor, not a universal low-biomass threshold.

3. Index-hopping evidence
-------------------------

File: 07-index-hopping-evidence.tsv

Peer-reviewed source:

- Costello M et al. Characterization and remediation of sample index swaps by
  non-redundant dual indexing on massively parallel sequencing platforms.
  BMC Genomics. 2018;19:332.
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1186/s12864-018-4703-0
  License: CC BY 4.0.

Current vendor guidance checked 2026-07-19:

- https://www.illumina.com/techniques/sequencing/ngs-library-prep/
  multiplexing/index-hopping.html

The reported ranges and the 1.59% worked example describe different library,
instrument and handling contexts. They are plotted together only to show why
no universal percentage should be used as a pass/fail threshold. Unique dual
indexes allow unexpected index pairs to be identified and filtered; decontam
does not replace index-pair, lane, donor-library or demultiplexing audits.
