Article 18 data and database notice
===================================

Benchmark input
---------------
The positive benchmark is the Illumina run ERR9765746 from BioProject
PRJEB52977 / BioSample SAMEA14435832, published with the 71-strain MOCK1 DNA
benchmark in Meslier et al. (2022), DOI 10.1038/s41597-022-01762-z. The study
and associated data are distributed under CC BY 4.0. Article 18 reads the same
99,991 synchronized, fastp-clean pairs already audited in Article 13; raw and
clean FASTQ remain outside Git. Exact mate checksums, record counts, base
counts, and the normalized pair-ID hash are recorded in
18-source-manifest.tsv.

Truth definition
----------------
Expected composition comes from Meslier et al. Supplementary Table S3. The
71 assembly rows and their printed genome percentages are preserved without
silent renormalization in 17-mock1-truth.tsv. Current NCBI species ancestors
and assembly metadata are the checksum-locked 2026-07-21 snapshots created for
Article 17. The 71 assemblies resolve to 69 current NCBI species.

Profiler evidence reused from earlier articles
-----------------------------------------------
MetaPhlAn 4.2.5 uses mpa_vJan26_CHOCOPhlAnSGB_202605 (Article 15). Kraken2
2.17.1 and Bracken package 3.1p1 use Standard-8-20260626 for the native
confidence-0 branch (Article 16); Standard-16-20260626, confidence 0.10 and
minimum hit groups 2 are retained as the control-aware sensitivity branch
(Article 17). Their large archives, indexes, per-read output, and mapout remain
outside Git; Article 18 reuses checksum-locked profiles and audit tables.

mOTUs software and marker-gene database
---------------------------------------
mOTUs 4.1.0 is from https://github.com/motu-tool/mOTUs/releases/tag/4.1.0.
The marker-gene database 4.1 archive is the immutable file in Zenodo record
20322482 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20322482): 5,552,983,255 bytes, publisher MD5
471ea128f0c0839f5c4629b949ea5f8a, and locally computed SHA-256
7d2d6382ecf766b23ef362311715cd612243af82c00c936da4597afb4e4df375.
The database version file reports release 4.1 dated 2026-01-26. Both shipped
taxonomy tables explicitly report GTDB R226. This takes precedence over stale
R220 wording found in some surrounding documentation. The archive and 9.70 GB
installed index remain under ignored database storage; only profiles,
crosswalks, normalized logs, aggregate resource evidence, and file checksums
are committed.

Comparison boundary
-------------------
MetaPhlAn SGBs, NCBI species taxa used by Kraken/Bracken, and GTDB-R226 mOTUs
are not interchangeable features. MetaPhlAn estimated mapped reads, Kraken
paired-fragment classifications, Bracken redistributed estimates, and mOTUs
marker-gene insert-scaled counts also do not share a denominator. Article 18
therefore keeps native output separate and limits abundance agreement to a
strict one-to-one common species domain established from database taxids and
exact assembly accessions. Exact taxonomy-name agreement may annotate a row,
but fuzzy or name-only matching never establishes strict reference presence.

Large-file policy
-----------------
FASTQ, MetaPhlAn/Kraken/mOTUs database archives and indexes, BAM, MGC, marker
mapout, and Kraken per-fragment output are intentionally excluded from Git.
Routine QA is network-free and reads only the checksum-locked frozen bundle.
