Article 19 data and database provenance
=======================================

Biological input
----------------
The biological input is the 99,991-pair clean subset of ERR9765746 prepared
and validated in Article 13. ERR9765746 is MOCK1 from Meslier et al. 2022
(PRJEB52977, SAMEA14435832). The source study distributes its data under
CC-BY-4.0. Article 19 does not redistribute raw FASTQ; it records the two
compressed-file hashes, record counts, base counts, and paired-ID hash in
19-source-manifest.tsv.

MetaPhlAn compatibility profile
--------------------------------
HUMAnN 3.9 accepts MetaPhlAn 3 profiles and MetaPhlAn 4 profiles containing
the vJun23 database signature. The primary run therefore generates a new
profile from the same clean FASTQ with MetaPhlAn 4.2.5 and
mpa_vJun23_CHOCOPhlAnSGB_202403. Both MetaPhlAn archives have publisher MD5
sidecars. The Article 15 vJan26 profile is retained only for a cross-release
taxon-selection audit; its header is never rewritten and HUMAnN is not patched.
HUMAnN 3.9 still checks the MetaPhlAn executable even when a profile is
supplied. MetaPhlAn 4.2.5 prints an additional installed-database status line
after its canonical version line, which the legacy HUMAnN parser mistakes for
the version. scripts/humann39-compat-bin/metaphlan exposes only that first line
for --version and delegates every non-version invocation unchanged. The wrapper
is checksum-locked with the frozen evidence; --bypass-prescreen is not used.

HUMAnN databases
----------------
The nucleotide database is full ChocoPhlAn v201901_v31 and the translated
database is the full annotated UniRef90 v201901b DIAMOND build. Their official,
release-specific endpoints expose exact Content-Length values but no checksum
sidecars as checked on 2026-07-22. Initial acquisition is therefore labelled a
retrieval lock, not publisher-checksum verification: HTTPS headers, effective
URL, byte count, tar/gzip integrity, local SHA-256, extraction inventory, and
per-file SHA-256 are recorded. After the first acquisition, the manifest is
changed to fail closed on the observed exact byte count and SHA-256.
The Globus endpoint advertises byte ranges but does not expose a usable total
length to aria2's ordinary GET path. The acquisition script therefore divides
the HEAD-verified Content-Length into 16 non-overlapping HTTP ranges, requires
HTTP 206 plus the exact byte count for every segment, and assembles the archive
in order before the whole-file integrity and SHA-256 checks.

Reaction regroup mapping
------------------------
The sensitivity branch uses HUMAnN 3.9's packaged
metacyc_reactions_level4ec_only.uniref.bz2 mapping through
humann_regroup_table --groups uniref90_rxn. The installed file is 57,511,575
bytes with SHA-256
8419ce78a62ca9130914f2c347a9708111cedc7de52ba274659ce51ec7de7752.
Regrouping is applied by sum to native gene-family RPK with UNGROUPED and
protected rows retained; reaction CPM and relative abundance are derived only
after regrouping. Because one UniRef90 cluster can map to multiple reactions,
regrouped totals can exceed their source totals and are not read counts.

Large artifacts
---------------
MetaPhlAn and HUMAnN archives, extracted databases, concatenated FASTQ,
Bowtie2 indexes, per-read alignments, and DIAMOND intermediates remain under
the Git-ignored db/humann-cache/ and data/raw/article19/ locations. Only small
tables, normalized logs, aggregate evidence, and checksum manifests are kept
under data/small/19-humann3-frozen/.
