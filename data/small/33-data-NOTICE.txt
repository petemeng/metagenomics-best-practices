Article 33 data, software, and interpretation notice
===================================================

Biological source and publication anchor
----------------------------------------
The biological inputs are real assemblies from Meslier et al., Benchmarking
second and third-generation sequencing platforms for microbial metagenomics,
Scientific Data 9:694 (2022), DOI 10.1038/s41597-022-01762-z (CC BY 4.0).
MOCK1 contains 71 strains and MOCK2 contains 87 strains. The official author
repository and reference genomes are locked at commit
a429a3724d4593f35b8d7323b20252a6be90e1cd. The chapter anchors its evaluation
to the study's Table 3 and Supplementary Table S2 but redraws all figures from
the newly computed QUAST/MetaQUAST tables.

Assembly inputs and non-duplication policy
------------------------------------------
Fifteen biological assembly branches are selected from checksum-locked
Article 30, 31, and 32 intermediate bundles. They comprise six short-read
single/co assemblies, four long-read assemblies, and five non-duplicated
short/hybrid/polished assemblies. The Flye ONT and Flye HiFi sequences present
in both Article 31 and Article 32 are included once, from Article 31, after
their compressed hashes were shown to agree across bundles. No FASTQ is read
and no assembly is rerun for Article 33.

Each selected compressed FASTA must appear in its upstream
file-checksums.sha256 and match that digest before it is decompressed. The
computed input-lineage table records source path, compressed SHA-256,
canonical record-and-sequence SHA-256, record count, total bases, length
statistics, GC, N bases, and IUPAC violations. The new frozen bundle stores
the compact lineage and QC evidence rather than duplicating all 15 large
FASTA files.

Truth sets and taxonomy aliases
-------------------------------
Supplementary Table S1 and the official MOCK_001.list/MOCK_002.list define
membership. The audit requires 71 MOCK1 and 87 MOCK2 accessions and verifies
that MOCK1 is a strict subset of MOCK2. Per-genome decompressed sequence-hash
multisets must exactly equal MOCK_001.fasta.gz and MOCK_002.fasta.gz. Twelve
explicit current-name to repository-name aliases are retained; aliases are
file-lineage corrections, not changes to the underlying reference sequence.

The two co-assemblies contain equal selected read-pair budgets from MOCK1 and
MOCK2. Their reference universe is the union of both truth sets, which equals
MOCK2 membership. For abundance-bin diagnostics only, co-assembly expected
abundance is the arithmetic mean of MOCK1 and MOCK2 percentages with NP
treated as zero. This operational value is not an independently measured DNA
concentration or realized sequencing coverage.

Deterministic diagnostic controls
---------------------------------
Two controls are derived from the real Flye HiFi assembly with seed namespace
20260733. They are diagnostic transformations, never biological workflows.

1. The fragmented control splits every contig deterministically at 50,000 bp;
   a terminal remainder below 1,000 bp is merged into the preceding piece.
   It preserves every base but lowers N50.
2. The chimeric control selects the 20 longest eligible contigs by the fixed
   rule length-descending/name-ascending and rotates one central 100,000-bp
   block among them. It preserves the total base multiset and the complete
   contig-length multiset, so N50 is unchanged while reference adjacency is
   deliberately damaged.

These controls isolate metric behavior. They cannot be counted when ranking
assemblers, estimating biological recovery, or claiming a platform effect.

Locked evaluation contract
--------------------------
QUAST/MetaQUAST 5.3.0 uses its bundled minimap2 2.28-r1209. All assemblies are
evaluated at a common >=1,000-bp contig threshold. MetaQUAST uses minimum
alignment 500 bp, identity >=97%, fragmented-reference mode, and the original
study's split-scaffolds sensitivity. Primary rows always use the original
assembly labels; *_broken rows remain a separate sensitivity table.

MOCK1, MOCK2, and MOCK1+MOCK2 are separate MetaQUAST runs because their truth
universes differ. Genome fraction and recovered-genome proportions may be
compared only with their truth-set denominator visible. Missing physical
per-reference reports are expanded into explicit zero-recovery rows;
mismatch and indel rates remain NA when no aligned denominator exists.

Interpretation boundary
-----------------------
N50 and contig counts describe a length distribution, not biological
correctness, completeness, contamination, or strain resolution. NA50 breaks
aligned sequence at extensive misassemblies and therefore answers a different
question. Genome fraction, mismatch/indel rate, duplication ratio,
misassemblies, unaligned sequence, read consistency, gene recovery, and MAG
quality remain separate evidence layers.

There is no universal usable-assembly threshold. Gene-centric analysis,
binning/MAG recovery, and complete-replicon claims require different minimum
lengths and orthogonal checks. In natural communities without exact truth,
reference-free QUAST must be paired with read-back consistency, coverage,
gene/marker recovery, assembly-graph inspection, and downstream MAG/contig
quality checks. A nearby public reference is not automatically ground truth;
biological structural variation can otherwise be misclassified as an
assembly error.
