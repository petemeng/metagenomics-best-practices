1. Marotz saliva host-depletion benchmark
-----------------------------------------

Source:
  Marotz CA, Sanders JG, Zuniga C, Zaramela LS, Knight R, Zengler K.
  Improving saliva shotgun metagenomics by chemical host DNA depletion.
  Microbiome. 2018;6:42.
  DOI: 10.1186/s40168-018-0426-3
  PMCID: PMC5827986

The six method-level values in 06-marotz-host-depletion-source.tsv are a
machine-readable transcription of the host-aligned read percentages reported
in the Results paragraph accompanying Figure 2. Eight participants contributed
triplicate aliquots to each processing method. The paper reports the values as
mean plus/minus a spread value but does not identify that spread in the Figure
2 caption; this repository therefore preserves the neutral column name
"reported_plusminus_pct" and does not relabel it as SD or SEM.

The reagent costs are the approximate per-sample consumable costs stated in the
Discussion. "NA" means that the paper did not provide a filtration cost.

available with it are covered by CC0 unless otherwise stated. Human-filtered
microbial reads are archived under ENA PRJEB24090 / ERP105893 and Qiita 11541.

2. Longhi saponin dose-response benchmark
-----------------------------------------

Source:
  Longhi G, Argentini C, Fontana F, et al.
  Saponin treatment for eukaryotic DNA depletion alters the microbial DNA
  profiles by reducing the abundance of Gram-negative bacteria in
  metagenomics analyses.
  Microbiome Research Reports. 2024;3:4.
  DOI: 10.20517/mrr.2023.02
  PMCID: PMC10917613

06-longhi-saponin-source.tsv joins:
  - Main Table 2: sequenced reads, high-quality reads, non-human retained reads,
    and the percentage removed as Homo sapiens for one sputum specimen; and
  - Supplementary Table 6: Gram-positive and Gram-negative percentages for the
    same untreated and saponin-dose conditions.

This is a seven-condition dose series on one sputum specimen, not seven
independent subjects. It demonstrates a protocol trade-off and must not be used
to estimate a population-average optimum. Raw shotgun data are archived under
SRA PRJNA864589.

3. Current computational-filter contract
----------------------------------------

06-host-filter-contract.tsv records two deliberately separate layers:
  - faithful reproduction of the 2018 Marotz processing versions; and
  - a current, privacy-aware short-read baseline using Hostile 2.0.2.

Hostile 2.0.2 and its standard-index composition were verified from the
official PyPI project page on 2026-07-19. The table does not claim that one
masked reference is universally optimal. A masked host reference retains more
microbial/viral homologous sequence but changes the privacy/false-negative
trade-off and therefore requires an independent residual-host audit.
