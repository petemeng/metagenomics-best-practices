Costea / McLaren shotgun mock-community inputs
----------------------------------------------

The files used to regenerate 05-costea-mock-profiles.tsv and
05-costea-bias-summary.tsv are downloaded from the archived analysis repository
for:

  McLaren MR, Willis AD, Callahan BJ. Consistent and correctable bias in
  metagenomic sequencing experiments. eLife. 2019;8:e46923.
  https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.46923

The underlying Phase III reads are from ENA study PRJEB14847. The archived
profiles were generated by McLaren et al. with MetaPhlAn2 2.7.6 using
--min_cu_len 0 --stat avg_g. The marker-database release is not encoded in the
RDA files and is therefore reported as unavailable rather than inferred.

The known mock-community cell composition originates from:

  Costea PI et al. Towards standards for human fecal sample processing in
  metagenomic studies. Nature Biotechnology. 2017;35:1069-1076.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/nbt.3960

synDNA mock benchmark
---------------------

05-syndna-mock-benchmark.tsv transcribes the eight bacterial rows and the
Mock (%), Pool 2 predicted (%), Pool 2 SD (%), relative-abundance predicted
(%), and relative-abundance SD (%) columns from Table 1 of:

  Zaramela LS, Tjuanta M, Moyne O, Neal M, Zengler K. synDNA-a Synthetic DNA
  Spike-in Method for Absolute Quantification of Shotgun Metagenomic
  Sequencing. mSystems. 2022;7:e00447-22.
  https://doi.org/10.1128/msystems.00447-22

The article and its table are distributed under CC BY 4.0. Values are retained
as published and are not renormalized to sum to 100%.
